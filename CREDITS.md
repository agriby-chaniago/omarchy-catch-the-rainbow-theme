## Optional full bundle

The full bundle is distributed separately as an opt-in release asset. Its Omarchy-derived components preserve David Heinemeier Hansson's MIT notice in `LICENSES/Omarchy-MIT.txt`.

The Oreo mask cursor is adapted from [Oreo Cursors by varlesh](https://github.com/varlesh/oreo-cursors), pinned to commit `7483f2dec06ae0fce182f9e9fe96c7db23b312b5`, under GPL-2.0. That asset is separately licensed. Its license, AUTHORS, change notice, editable SVGs, hotspot/animation metadata and build tooling are included in the full bundle. The bundle's MIT license does not replace the cursor's GPL license.

The paper icon set is a local adaptation for this theme. Application icon fallbacks are supplied by the user's Yaru installation; the Yaru application icon collection is not bundled here.
